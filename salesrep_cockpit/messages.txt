Why Retool (and where WordPress falls short)
WordPress worked as a quick way to display a table, but it’s now limiting us in several ways:

Performance & scale

The table is slow when filters/searching, especially as the dataset grows.

It’s not designed for heavy querying, complex joins, or large datasets like we need with SAQ data.

Data model & rules are hard to maintain

The “small rules” (mappings, exceptions, calculations) are embedded in WordPress logic/plugins and are not easy to audit, version, or expand.

When we want to add a rule or change a calculation, it becomes fragile and takes longer than it should.

Limited dashboard experience

WordPress tables don’t give us a real “cockpit” experience: KPIs, drill-downs, role-based views…
