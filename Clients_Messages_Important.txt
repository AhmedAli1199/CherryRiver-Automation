1. PURPOSE

We are building an Autonomous COO System inside Odoo 19 that continuously monitors, predicts and auto-corrects:

• Stock ruptures by sales territory
• Raw material & packaging availability
• Production feasibility (simulate any batch size)
• Automatic purchase order generation
• SAQ & IDFood logistics compliance
   •   Logistic warehouse scanning & verification stock and quantity 

This system must run continuously and generate a Weekly COO Command Report every Monday.

⸻

2. CORE COO QUESTIONS TO AUTOMATE

The system must be able to answer:
	1.	Were there any stock ruptures by sales territory last week SAQ stores number and products numbers 
	2.	Which SKUs were in rupture SAQ which store under the watch of which Representant and for how many days?
	3.	Which store for what product under the watch of which Representant  are currently under minimum stock?
	4.	What replenishment orders must be generated?
	5.	Which raw materials & packaging must be ordered now?
	6.	What will be in rupture in 14 / 30 days?
	8.	Are all inbound Tropo pallets/cases scanned, counted, and located?
	9.	Are SAQ / IDFood orders fully prepared with BOL and compliance documents?
	10.	Can we simulate production of X liters of any product and automatically generate missing purchase orders?

⸻
